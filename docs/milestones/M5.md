## 1. Summary of What Was Built

1. **Evidence Player Component (`web/src/components/EvidencePlayer.tsx`):**
   - HTML5 video player synchronized with an overlay HTML5 `<canvas>`.
   - `requestAnimationFrame` render loop synchronizing bounding boxes and suspicious region heatmap gradients with video playback.
   - Comprehensive controls: Play/Pause (`Space`), frame step backward/forward (`ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight`), jump to peak suspicious clip (`P`), toggle heatmap overlay (`H`), and opacity slider.
   - Multi-track face selector to toggle focus between detected faces.

2. **Clip Probability Timeline (`web/src/components/ClipTimeline.tsx`):**
   - Recharts area/step chart showing clip probability vs. time per track.
   - Horizontal threshold line at $\tau$ (decision band boundary).
   - Shaded sampling windows for long video protocol (initial 8s + spaced 32-frame windows).
   - Interactive seek points: clicking any point on the chart seeks the video player.
   - Real-time vertical cursor tracking video playhead.

3. **Review Workspace Route (`web/src/routes/ReviewWorkspace.tsx`):**
   - Split-screen triage interface: Evidence Player + Clip Timeline on the left; Decision Panel on the right.
   - Keyboard triage shortcuts:
     - `1`: Likely Authentic
     - `2`: Likely Manipulated
     - `3`: Inconclusive / Cannot Tell
     - `Enter`: Submit review decision and immediately load next queue item
     - `R`: Reset form
     - `S`: Skip case
   - Checkbox reason codes: Blending seam, Mouth/teeth, Temporal flicker, Lighting, Identity mismatch.
   - Telemetry instrumentation: `time_to_decision_ms` and `overlay_toggled_count`.

4. **Queue Dashboard & Case Detail Routes (`web/src/routes/Dashboard.tsx`, `CaseDetail.tsx`):**
   - Dashboard with queue metrics, filterable table, search, and "Start Review Queue" quick action.
   - Case Detail view with media container metadata, model cascade results, and cryptographic receipt details.

5. **Copy & Accessibility Compliance (`web/src/copy/forbidden.ts`):**
   - Zero bare "FAKE"/"REAL" labels in the UI.
   - Mandatory inclusion of screening aid disclaimer: *"Screening aid — not proof. Human review required for consequential action."*
   - WCAG 2.2 AA compliant typography, focus outlines, and keyboard shortcuts.
