## 1. Summary of What Was Built

1. **Video Decoding & Sampling (`sv_engine.preprocess.decode`):**
   - Implemented PyAV frame decoding with presentation timestamp (PTS) tracking.
   - Handled video rotation metadata (90°, 180°, 270°) normalizing all frames upright.
   - Dual sampling protocol conforming to SPEC §2.3:
     - Videos $\le 30$ seconds: all frames decoded.
     - Videos $> 30$ seconds: initial 8 seconds + 4 spaced 32-frame sampling windows.
   - Exception handling via `DecodeError` for corrupt or unreadable files.

2. **Face Detection & Tracking (`sv_engine.preprocess.faces`, `tracks`):**
   - SCRFD face detector wrapper and deterministic `MockFaceDetector` fallback.
   - Multi-face greedy IoU tracker ($\ge 0.3$) capped at `max_tracks=4`.
   - 5-point facial landmark smoothing across temporal tracks using SciPy Savitzky-Golay filters.

3. **Affine Alignment, Normalization & Clipping (`sv_engine.preprocess.align`, `clips`):**
   - 5-point ArcFace similarity transformation warping face crops to $256 \times 256$.
   - Deterministic center crop to $224 \times 224$ with $M_{224}$ back-projection matrix storage.
   - Formation of $K=8$ contiguous clips of $T=8$ frames using `contiguous_starts`.

4. **Inconclusive Evaluation & Serialization (`sv_engine.preprocess.clips`):**
   - Short-circuit classification for `DECODE_FAILURE`, `NO_FACE`, `FACE_TOO_SMALL` (median IOD $< 24$ px), and `TOO_FEW_FRAMES` ($< 8$ frames).
   - NPZ archive serialization (`save_tracks_npz`, `load_tracks_npz`) saving uint8 frames, landmarks, detector confidences, and affine matrices.
